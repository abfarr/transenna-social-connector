# Setting up your social post system

You'll get a private connector link from us. It looks like `https://.../mcp/abc123...`. Keep it to yourself, since anyone with it can use your access.

Setup takes about 10 minutes, then onboarding takes about 30 to 60 minutes of back and forth with the AI.

## 1. Gather what you have

Any mix of these is fine. More gives a better result.

- Your brand style guide, if you have one
- Your website link
- Your social account handles
- A few past posts or captions you're proud of
- Your logo files and a folder of your best photos
- Screenshots of a few accounts whose look you like
- If you have them, screenshots of your post insights (top posts, reach)

## 2. Add the connector

### In Claude

1. Go to **Customize → Connectors**.
2. Click **+ Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
3. Name it, paste your connector link, and click **Continue**.
4. For sign-in, choose **No sign in**, then click **Add**.

On a Team or Enterprise plan, your workspace owner adds it under **Organization settings → Connectors**, and you then click **Connect**.

### In ChatGPT

1. Go to **Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings** and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Back in **Apps & Connectors**, click **Create**.
3. Name it, paste your connector link, choose **No authentication**, and save.

Menu names in both apps change from time to time. If you can't find these, tell us and we'll walk you through it.

## 3. Create a project

Create a new project in your AI app for your social posts. Add your photo and branding folder to it.

## 4. Start onboarding

In a new chat in that project, with the connector turned on, say:

> Start my social post onboarding.

Share what you gathered when it asks. It will review your current social accounts, ask a few questions about anything it couldn't find, and then give you your files.

## 5. Finish setup

Onboarding ends with a setup checklist. In short:

1. Add the files it gave you (Client Onboarding Brief, Example Captions, Template Index) to your project.
2. Send the Step 1, Step 2 and Step 3 prompts in order, each in your project, following the checklist.
3. Paste the project instructions into your project's instructions.

After that, ask for posts in any chat in the project.
