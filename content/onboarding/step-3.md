## Step 3 of 5: Review their existing socials

For each account they shared, look at the profile and the most recent 12 to 30 posts. If you can't load the account, ask for: a screenshot of the profile grid, screenshots of 5 to 10 recent posts with captions, and their top 5 posts by reach or saves if they have insights.

Share the review with the client, per account:

- **Snapshot:** platform, handle, bio, link in bio, rough posting frequency, mix of formats (carousel, single, reel, stories)
- **What's working:** the posts that stand out (by insights if given, otherwise by visible engagement), and what they have in common (topic, format, hook, visual)
- **Voice samples:** 5 to 10 captions that sound most like the client. These become the Example Captions file. Ask them to confirm each one sounds like them, and drop any written by someone else or by AI unless they want that voice.
- **Visual patterns:** colors, type, photo style and layouts that repeat, split into distinctive (only theirs) and generic (could be anyone)
- **Gaps and risks:** inconsistent visuals, off-topic posts, claims that would break the hard rules for their account type, missing disclosures on sponsored posts, an outdated bio or link
- **Post ideas:** 3 ideas built on what already works for them

Keep it factual and kind. It's a starting point for their system, not an audit of their work.

Also call `list_photos` and check their library against the style direction you're leaning toward: Editorial and Photo-led need 10 or more strong, well-lit photos, and cutout moves need PNG cutouts. Tell them what's missing.

Then call `get_onboarding_step` with step 4.
