## Step 4 of 5: Fill the gaps with a short interview

Compare what you've collected against the blanks list from the start of onboarding. Then:

- Confirm, don't re-ask. Put the facts you inferred in one short list: "Here's what I took from your materials. Correct anything that's wrong."
- Ask only about blanks that are still empty, one topic at a time, no more than 3 short questions per message.
- Never fill in a fact they haven't given or confirmed. Anything still missing at the end stays as [CONFIRM: ...].
- Offers, prices, promotions, credentials, results and testimonials always need the client's explicit confirmation, even if they appear on the website.

When the blanks are filled or marked [CONFIRM], call `get_onboarding_step` with step 5.
