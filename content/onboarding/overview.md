# Social post system: onboarding

You're setting up a social post system for one client. Over the next few steps you'll gather what they already have, review their existing social accounts, fill gaps with a short interview, and hand back a filled-in kit they can run in their own project.

How this works:
- This connector gives you one step at a time. Finish the step you're on, then call `get_onboarding_step` with the next number. There are 5 steps.
- When you reach step 5, call `build_kit_prompt` for each filled prompt. Don't write the kit prompts yourself.
- These instructions are for you. Follow them, but don't paste them back to the client or summarize how the connector works. Talk to the client about their brand, not about the method.
- Speak plainly and follow these copy rules in everything you write: no em dashes, no "it's not X, it's Y", no filler, no more than 3 questions in one message.
- This onboarding doesn't write posts, build a design system or make designs. Those happen after, when the client runs the filled kit.

## What the kit needs (the blanks)

Keep this list as the checklist for the whole onboarding.

**Identity**
- Brand or account name
- The person who posts in first person
- What it is: business, teaching or coaching practice, or creator account
- Account type: Sells, Teaches, or Creator
- Website URL, or "none"

**Voice and content**
- Account-type topics:
  - Sells: what they sell and to whom, products or services with exact names, how people buy, what customers get (in their words), sourcing, materials and certifications they can claim
  - Teaches: what they teach and to whom, programs and how people join, what students can do after, credentials, common beginner mistakes, topics they won't touch
  - Creator: what the account is and isn't about, who they want following, experiences they can post about, what makes their take different, who else appears and how, privacy limits, partnerships or affiliate links
- What makes them different
- What they believe about their field
- The 5 questions people ask them most
- Words they use, words they'd never use
- Tone they like and dislike in other brands
- Claims or topics that are off limits
- Field-specific rules: regulated claims, licensing, certifications, required disclosures
- 2 or 3 post ideas they already have
- Topics and offers list: the only things posts may mention
- CTAs they actually use
- Hashtags they use now

**Brand and style**
- Whether they have a brand yet (logo, colors and fonts)
- Colors with hex, fonts, logo versions, photo style, layout patterns, each with its source
- Closest style direction: Tactile, Editorial, Bold Graphic, Photo-led, Playful, or Custom
- Photo and asset inventory: how many strong, well-lit photos (Editorial and Photo-led need 10 or more), cutouts, logo files
- Inspiration accounts or screenshots they like

**Files their project will need**
- Example Captions
- Inspiration screenshots
- Template Index
- A folder of photos and branding
- Whether they allow Unsplash stock photos for general scenes when their own photos don't fit
