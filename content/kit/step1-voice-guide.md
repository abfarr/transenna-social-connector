DESIGN TEXT:
Make it look like {{name}}, not like AI made it.

Style direction: my design system names one main direction from the five profiles below (or a Custom profile defined in this section). Depth and layering: posts should never feel flat, but how much layering they need depends on my direction (see its profile). A layer means one thing sits partly over another. A mask, a thin rule, a big headline or a tight crop on its own is a detail, not a layer. A photo on top with text underneath and nothing crossing between them is flat.

Shared, any direction:
- Cutouts that break the frame: a product, object, person or brand illustration with the background removed, overlapping a slide edge, a color block or the headline's band. A shadow under the cutout only if my direction's profile allows shadows.
- Overlap between layers: a photo or illustration sitting partly over a color block, with type on the block
- Text on a photo: optional. Only on a calm, even area of the image, and only when the text clearly passes contrast (4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for headlines 72px and up). No scrim or gradient to force it unless my direction's profile allows one. If it doesn't pass, move the text to flat ground.

Fonts named in the profiles are starting points from Google Fonts. If I have brand fonts, use mine when they fit the profile. Otherwise pick for fit with my world, not because a font is common.

Tactile (handmade, real objects):

Editorial (magazine feature, refined):

Headlines 60 to 84px, up to 120px on the cover, never negative letter spacing. All caps works for headlines of 4 words or fewer. An optional script accent (Pinyon Script, Allura) once per carousel.
- Color: one light ground, one dark ground and one accent used sparingly. At most two colors per slide besides photos.
- Photos: large and straight, with clean edges, no frames and no shadows. When two photos appear together, pair a wide shot with a detail.
- Density: margins of 96px or more. At most 3 elements and 25 words of body per slide. One slide is almost empty. Keep a strict grid and break it at most once per carousel.

- Moves:
- Inset photo: a second photo set over a full-bleed photo, crossing off its edge onto the color ground; straight, clean edges, no rotation, no tape
- Type across an edge: the headline starts on the color ground and runs over the edge of a photo, passing contrast on both
- Stacked photos: two or three photos overlapping in a staggered stack, straight, no frames
- Numeral behind: a giant numeral or word in a tint of the ground, partly hidden behind a photo or cutout
- Details that add polish but don't count as layers: arch and circle masks, thin rules, hairline frames, scale contrast, generous empty space, a pull quote in italic serif
